Welcome to the Journal

If you're reading this, you've probably picked up a journal at some point, written enthusiastically for three days, and then watched it gather dust on a shelf. You're not alone, and this blog exists for exactly that person: you, before dust.

Why guided?

A blank page is a question mark. A guided page is a gentle hand on your shoulder saying, "start here." Guided journaling doesn't mean you're not writing your own thoughts - it just removes the friction between you and an honest sentence.

What you'll find here

  • Weekly prompts that fit real life
  • Practical habit advice, no hustle attached
  • Honest posts about mental health and the writing life

How to start this week

Don't wait for Monday. Tonight, write one sentence about today - one thing that happened, one thing you felt. That's a complete practice. Do it again tomorrow, and you're doing it.
