10 Prompts for Hard Days

On hard days, "how are you feeling?" is a trap - the answer is too big and too tangled. Here are ten gentler questions to write about instead. Pick one, any one. Give it five minutes.

  1. What is one thing that felt okay today?
  2. What do I wish someone would say to me right now?
  3. What is taking up the most space in my head, in one sentence?
  4. What would I say to a friend going through this exact day?
  5. What did I do today that a younger me would be proud of?
  6. What is one thing I can let go of - just for tonight?
  7. What small comfort am I allowed to give myself?
  8. Who or what am I grateful for, even in the middle of this?
  9. What would "enough" look like today - not perfect, just enough?
  10. If this feeling had a voice, what would it be saying to me?

One rule

Don't fix anything in your entry. Hard days aren't for solving problems; they're for naming them. The journal is a place to set things down, not to sort them. Set them down, close the page, and be done.
